Teleprompter Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Teleprompter Pro does not collect, store or share your personal data on any server. Your scripts, settings, history and recordings stay on your device.

This policy explains how the Teleprompter Pro mobile app (“the app”) handles information. The app is developed and published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”).

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads and no analytics. The app contains no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • No servers of ours. We do not operate a backend for the app, so nothing you create is sent to us.
  • One online feature. The optional Wikipedia search sends your search words to Wikipedia (see Third-party services).

Information stored on your device

Everything below is created by you and kept only in the app’s private storage or your device’s photo gallery. We cannot see or access it.

InformationWhere it is keptHow to remove it
Scripts you type, paste, import or take from WikipediaApp storageClear the text field, or delete the app
Saved scripts (title and text)App database on the deviceDelete them in Scripts, or delete the app
Session history (date, time, duration, script title, first 100 characters of the script)App database on the deviceDelete entries in History, or delete the app
Settings (language, theme, speed, font, colors)App preferences on the deviceDelete the app
Video recordingsYour device’s photo galleryDelete them in the gallery or in Recordings

Deleting the app removes everything in app storage. Videos already saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them.

Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can turn any permission off in your device settings.

PermissionWhy the app uses it
CameraTo show the camera preview and record video in Record video mode. Not used in Practice mode.
MicrophoneTo record audio with your video. Sound levels may also be measured on the device to adjust scrolling; audio is never sent anywhere.
Photos / galleryTo save your recordings to the gallery and to open them for playback or sharing.
FilesTo import a .txt or .md script you pick. Only the file you choose is read.
InternetTo search and load Wikipedia articles.

Location: the app never uses your location. On iOS a location description is present only because the platform requires it for some system frameworks; the app never requests this permission.

Third-party services

The app talks to outside services only when you choose a feature that needs them.

  • Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation). When you search Wikipedia or open an article, the app sends your search words or the article title, your app language and a standard app identifier to wikipedia.org. Article images load from upload.wikimedia.org. Like any website, Wikimedia receives your IP address. This is governed by the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy. We never receive this data.
  • Sharing. When you share a recording, a script or the app, your device’s share menu opens and you choose where it goes. That app’s own policy then applies.
  • Links and email. Privacy policy, terms, store and “Contact us” links open in your browser, app store or email app. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.
  • App stores. Downloads and ratings are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own policies.

Retention and security

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. It is protected by your device’s own security, such as its lock screen and encryption. Device backups made by Apple or Google may include app data under their policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone. Parents who believe a child has emailed us personal information can contact us to have it deleted.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data from the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may still contact us with any privacy request, and we will answer it. Emails you send us are deleted on request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it at this address and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
